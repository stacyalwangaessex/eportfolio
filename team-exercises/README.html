Development Team Project: Pampered Pets risk identification report (Group 2)

Artefact: Pampered_Pets_Risk_Identification_Report_2.docx (original submission) · Full text

The task. Group 2 acted as a security consultancy for Pampered Pets, a four-person micro-enterprise facing an investor's proposal to digitalise and to source internationally, producing a risk identification report under ISO/IEC 27005:2022 covering the status quo (R1-R6) and the digitalisation programme (D1-D6), with a phased, baseline-first recommendation.

My individual contribution. I assisted the risk evaluation stage: scoring the identified risks against the five-point likelihood/impact matrix in both assessments, and shaping the evaluation judgements behind the recommendation. The team decided by consensus, jointly agreeing that none of the investor's three headline figures (50% revenue uplift, 24% cost saving, 33% attrition) should be accepted as stated.

Supporting evidence. The submitted report itself; no separate documentary evidence is held.
