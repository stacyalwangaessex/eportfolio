Meeting notes — Development Team Project (Group 2)

The Group 2 consultancy work for the Pampered Pets risk identification report was conducted through the university's online learning platform: task division, drafting and review took place in the group workspace, with decisions agreed by consensus.

Formal minutes were not retained for individual meetings. The team's decisions are nonetheless evidenced in the submitted report itself:

  • The adoption of ISO/IEC 27005:2022 over rejected alternatives (NIST SP 800-30, OCTAVE Allegro) records the group's methodology decision.
  • The consensus that none of the investor's three headline figures (50% revenue uplift, 24% cost saving, 33% attrition) should be accepted as stated is reflected in the recommendations.
  • The phased, baseline-first implementation recommendation records the group's agreed course of action.
