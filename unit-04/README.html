Unit 4: Application of Threat Modelling and Management Techniques

Unit topic: Applying threat modelling in practice (threat modelling manifesto, OWASP cookbook, MITRE ATT&CK), evaluating available tools and creating threat models (OWASP, 2024).

Artefact(s)

  • STRIDE threat model and attack trees, applied in the individual project: Executive summary §2, Risk Identification

Link to learning outcomes

The unit's outcomes (select the appropriate modelling tool and critically evaluate it) are evidenced by the executive summary's method justification: STRIDE chosen because it addresses how a system could be attacked rather than how it might fail (OWASP, 2024), with the top threat decomposed using attack trees. The skill carried through to the final assessment.

References

OWASP (2024) Threat Modeling Cheat Sheet. Available at: https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Threat_Modeling_Cheat_Sheet.html (Accessed: 11 October 2026).
