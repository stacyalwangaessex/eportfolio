Unit 4 — Application of Threat Modelling and Management Techniques

Unit topic: Applying threat modelling and management in practice, guided by the threat modelling manifesto, the OWASP cookbook and the MITRE ATT&CK database: evaluating publicly available tools, and creating threat models for suggested scenarios (OWASP, 2024).

Artefact(s)

  • STRIDE threat model and attack trees on the automated warehousing estate — applied again at module scale in the individual project: Executive summary §2, Risk Identification

Link to learning outcomes

The Unit 4 outcome set — select the appropriate modelling tool for a situation, and critically evaluate it — is evidenced by the executive summary's method justification: STRIDE was selected because it addresses how a system could be attacked rather than merely how it might fail (OWASP, 2024), and the highest-impact threat (disruption of an automated warehouse) was then decomposed with attack trees. This shows the unit's core skill — combining frameworks into a hybrid model — carried through from classroom scenario to the final assessment.
