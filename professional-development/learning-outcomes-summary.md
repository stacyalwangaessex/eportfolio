# Summary of Learning Outcomes

**LO1 — Identify and analyse critically security risks, threats and vulnerabilities in information systems, accounting for the current threat landscape.**
Evidence: [risk identification in the executive summary §2 (STRIDE + attack trees, two risk clusters)](../unit-11-individual-project/executive_summary.md); [team exercise outputs](../team-exercises/README.md).

**LO2 — Gather and synthesise information from multiple sources (including internet security alerts and warning sites) to aid systematic analysis of risks and security issues.**
Evidence: [forum contributions drawing on current threat/alert sources](../discussion-forums/README.md); [sector surveys (BCI 2023, PwC 2023) used to inform modelled assumptions in §4](../unit-11-individual-project/executive_summary.md).

**LO3 — Critically determine appropriate methodologies, tools and techniques to mitigate and/or solve security risks and their business impact.**
Evidence: [method selection with justification and rejected alternatives (§3), reproducible Monte Carlo model](../unit-11-individual-project/poc_execsummary_mc_c8f2.py), [prioritised recommendations and DR/BCP design (§6-7)](../unit-11-individual-project/executive_summary.md).

**LO4 — Articulate the legal, social, ethical and professional issues faced by information security and risk professionals.**
Evidence: [GDPR/DPIA/international-transfer analysis and the ethics paragraph (§6)](../unit-11-individual-project/executive_summary.md); [forum posts on legal/ethical issues](../discussion-forums/README.md).

**Professional skills developed:** see the [Skills Matrix and PDP](skills-matrix-pdp.md).
