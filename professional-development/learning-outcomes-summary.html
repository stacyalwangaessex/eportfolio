Summary of Learning Outcomes

LO1 — Identify and analyse critically security risks, threats and vulnerabilities in information systems, accounting for the current threat landscape. Evidence: risk identification in the executive summary §2 (STRIDE + attack trees, two risk clusters); team exercise outputs.

LO2 — Gather and synthesise information from multiple sources (including internet security alerts and warning sites) to aid systematic analysis of risks and security issues. Evidence: forum contributions drawing on current threat/alert sources; sector surveys (BCI 2023, PwC 2023) used to inform modelled assumptions in §4.

LO3 — Critically determine appropriate methodologies, tools and techniques to mitigate and/or solve security risks and their business impact. Evidence: method selection with justification and rejected alternatives (§3), reproducible Monte Carlo model, prioritised recommendations and DR/BCP design (§6-7).

LO4 — Articulate the legal, social, ethical and professional issues faced by information security and risk professionals. Evidence: GDPR/DPIA/international-transfer analysis and the ethics paragraph (§6); forum posts on legal/ethical issues.

Professional skills developed: see the Skills Matrix and PDP.
