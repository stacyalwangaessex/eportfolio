# Unit 01

**Unit topic:** Data and technology risks to fairness and accountability in human rights investigations (three-week discussion activity covering Units 1–3)

## Artefact(s)

- [Discussion forum — Initial Post](discussion-post-unit1-initial.html) (peer responses follow in Unit 2; summary post in Unit 3)

## Link to learning outcomes

The initial post analyses how data-driven investigative technologies create new risks to fairness and accountability in human rights investigations, arguing through a risk-management framing that such technology changes the risk profile rather than merely reducing it. It evidences LO4 (legal, social, ethical and professional issues: fairness, accountability, consent, surveillance) and LO2 (synthesis of multiple sources: FAccT research, the UN Berkeley Protocol and social-data scholarship).
