About Me

I am Stacy Alwanga, a Master's student in Cybersecurity at the University of Essex Online and an information security analyst. My work spans in-depth penetration testing of web and mobile applications, APIs and network endpoints (Nessus, Burp Suite, Metasploit, Kali Linux), social-engineering and phishing simulations, vulnerability assessments, risk assessments and gap analyses including DPIAs, rapid incident response for threats such as ransomware and malware, SIEM configuration (Wazuh), adversary emulation, and the delivery of corporate security training, always closing the loop with clear reporting and remediation guidance.
