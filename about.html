About Me

I am Stacy Alwanga, an information security analyst working across red-team engagements and security audits, studying for an MSc in Cybersecurity at the University of Essex Online.

Professional background

My day-to-day work centres on penetration testing, vulnerability assessments and security configuration reviews of networks and network assets, alongside red-team exercises that test defences under realistic conditions. The audit side of the role covers control frameworks and governance, including ISO/IEC 27005-style risk treatment and data-protection obligations such as UK GDPR and DPIAs. I enrolled on the MSc Cybersecurity to deepen the risk-management dimension of that practice: moving from finding vulnerabilities to quantifying and prioritising the risks they create for the business.

Why this e-portfolio

This e-portfolio collates the work I produced for the Security and Risk Management module (SRM_PCOM7E): discussion contributions, a team risk identification project, a quantitative individual risk assessment, an evaluation of both projects, and a reflective piece with my professional development plan.
