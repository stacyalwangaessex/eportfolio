Unit 5 — An Introduction to Security and Risk Standards in Industry and the Enterprise

Unit topic: The role of industry and enterprise standards in risk assessments and risk designs; exploring industry-specific standards and directives and selecting the appropriate standard(s) for a given situation.

Artefact(s)

  • Standards selection under real constraints in the Development Team Project: Pampered Pets risk identification report §2 — ISO/IEC 27005:2022 adopted over NIST SP 800-30 and OCTAVE Allegro, with Cyber Essentials as the proportionate benchmark
  • Enterprise-scale standards layering in the individual project: Executive summary §6 — ISO 31000, NIST CSF 2.0, UK/EU GDPR

Link to learning outcomes

The unit's outcome — list common security standards and select the appropriate one(s) for a given situation — is evidenced twice at different scales. In the team report, ISO/IEC 27005:2022 was chosen because its guidance-based, asset-driven approach scales down to a four-person firm, with Cyber Essentials adopted as a proportionate control benchmark and NIST SP 800-30/OCTAVE Allegro explicitly rejected with reasons. In the executive summary, the same selection discipline operates upwards: ISO 31000 and NIST CSF 2.0 frame an assurance model for a digitalising enterprise. Together these show the standard-follows-situation judgement the unit teaches.
